# Payment Terms
All Brightpath invoices are payable within 15 days of the invoice date. Payments can be made by bank transfer (NEFT/RTGS) or UPI using the details printed on the invoice.

# Late Payments
Invoices unpaid 15 days after the due date attract a late fee of 1.5 percent per month on the outstanding amount. Services may be paused if an invoice remains unpaid 45 days after the due date, after a final written notice.

# Payment Plans
Clients facing temporary cash-flow difficulty can request a payment plan by replying to any reminder. The accounts team responds within one business day and can split the outstanding amount into up to three instalments.
