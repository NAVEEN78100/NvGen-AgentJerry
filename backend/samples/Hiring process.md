# Interview Process
Shortlisted candidates are invited to a 45-minute video interview with the hiring manager and one team member. The interview covers past projects, a short practical discussion and time for the candidate's own questions.

# Before The Interview
Candidates receive a calendar invitation with a Google Meet link after confirming a slot. No preparation is required beyond reviewing the job description; designers are welcome to share a portfolio link.

# After The Interview
Every candidate hears back within five working days of the interview, whatever the outcome.
