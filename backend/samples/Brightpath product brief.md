# Brightpath Cloud Overview
Brightpath Cloud is an analytics and automation platform for growing businesses. It connects point-of-sale, inventory, ERP and spreadsheet data into one live dashboard, and sends alerts before problems become expensive. Most customers are live within 14 days, and onboarding includes a dedicated success manager.

# Retail
Retail teams use Brightpath to forecast demand store by store. Our festival-season forecasting model predicts stock-outs up to three weeks ahead, and retail customers typically reduce stock-outs by 30 percent in their first quarter. Online and in-store inventory are unified in a single view, so a product sold on the website is reserved from the nearest store automatically.

# Healthcare
Clinics, hospitals and pharmacy distributors use Brightpath to track medicine stock, batch expiry dates and bed occupancy. Expiry alerts are sent 60 days before a batch expires, which helped one 12-clinic network cut write-offs by 40 percent. All patient-identifiable data stays within Indian data centres and access is role-based.

# Logistics
Logistics companies use Brightpath for warehouse and fleet dashboards. Delayed shipments are flagged automatically when a delivery misses its promised window, and warehouse teams see inventory accuracy by zone every morning. Customers report a 22 percent improvement in on-time delivery within two months.

# Manufacturing
Manufacturers connect machine and ERP data to Brightpath to watch production lines in real time. Predictive maintenance alerts warn supervisors about likely machine downtime before it happens, and batch quality reports are generated automatically for compliance audits. Brightpath integrates with SAP, Tally and Zoho ERP out of the box.

# Hospitality
Restaurants, cafes and hotels use Brightpath to compare outlets side by side. Food cost and wastage reports show which dishes lose money, and restaurant groups typically cut wastage by 18 percent. Hotels get occupancy and weekend revenue insights to price rooms with confidence.

# Education
Schools, academies and coaching centres use Brightpath to understand enrolment, fee collection and batch performance. Drop-off reports highlight students who may leave before it happens, and parent-friendly progress summaries can be shared every week.
